v.1.0.2

Added

* Emberwrought Lamp — A dark medieval fantasy lantern with intricate ironwork and a fiery orange glow.
* Wrought Iron Lamp Post — An elegant street lamp featuring decorative wrought iron and warm lighting.
* EnableFlare Config — Enable or disable the orange flare effects surrounding lamp bulbs with a single setting.
* BulbBrightness Config — Adjust bulb emission brightness independently of the light illuminating the surroundings.
* CustomLampColor Config — Set a custom lamp color using a HEX color code.

Improved

* Improved lamp bulb illumination and color appearance.
* Flare settings now update immediately without restarting the game.
* Disabling flares preserves lamp lighting and selected colors.
* Improved handling of flare particle effects when lamps are switched off.

v.1.0.3

Added

* Wall-Mounted Viking Shield and Axes — A decorative Viking shield with crossed axes.
* Wall-Mounted Viking Shield Dragons — A wall-mounted shield with a dragon design.
* Wall-Mounted Viking Shield Ravens — A wall-mounted shield with a raven design.

Improved

* Reorganized all 64 prefab registrations into matching groups, including shields, lamps, beds, chairs, thrones, benches, rugs, and tea sets.
* Corrected duplicate and decimal registration numbering to a consecutive list from 1 to 64.
* Updated Valheim build-menu usage tags for Lighting, Furniture, Defense, and Decor.
* Simplified the configurable hammer category to a single CustomHammerTab option.
* Prepared optimized synchronization settings for stationary build pieces, matching Moonforged Gates & Fences. This update requires the separately revised RelicRegistrar.cs and still needs large-base multiplayer testing.

Fixed

* Fixed the Viking Chieftain's Bed interaction range to prevent interaction from excessive distances.

Adjusted Crafting Requirements

* FineWood Throne — Reduced FineWood from 20 to 10.
* Werewolf Throne — Replaced 5 Iron with 5 Silver.
* Geirrhafa's Throne — Replaced TrophyCultist_Hildir with TrophyCultist.
* Iron Throne — Replaced 10 Bronze with 10 BlackMetal.
* Ancient Root Bookshelf — Reduced FineWood from 20 to 2 and RoundLog from 5 to 2.
